Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how STANDING ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the STANDING iOS app, currently in beta.

Information we collect

To operate the STANDING group-dinner app, we collect:

  • Phone number — used to identify your account and send one-time login codes by SMS.
  • Name — so your dinner group knows who you are.
  • Dinner preferences — such as cuisine interests, availability windows, and settings you choose in the app, used to recommend restaurants for your group.
  • RSVPs — your responses to dinner invitations, used to coordinate the group.
  • Ratings — your post-dinner ratings and notes, used to improve future restaurant recommendations.

How we use your information

We use the information above solely to operate the STANDING app:

  • Sending one-time passcodes by SMS so you can log in securely.
  • Coordinating your group's dinners: proposals, invitations, reminders, and RSVP tracking.
  • Sending dinner-related notifications (for example, a confirmed reservation or a nudge to RSVP).
  • Improving restaurant recommendations based on your ratings and notes.

SMS data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your phone number is used only for account authentication and the dinner-coordination messages described above.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the service — for example, with our hosting and messaging providers — and never for those providers' own marketing.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You may ask us to delete your account and data at any time using the contact below.

Changes
