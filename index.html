STANDING is a mobile app for recurring group dinners. You pick your people and your rhythm — every two weeks, every month, whatever works — and STANDING handles the rest: finding a restaurant everyone will love, locking in the plan, and booking the table for you.

STANDING is built for friend groups, families, and colleagues who want to see each other regularly without the endless group-text negotiation that usually kills the plan.

STANDING is an app — this website is its home on the web

STANDING’s service is delivered entirely through our mobile app: STANDING — Plans on Repeat for iPhone (iOS), currently in private beta through Apple’s TestFlight program ahead of a public App Store launch. There is no separate web version of the product and no physical storefront — the app is the product.

This website (standingplans.app) is the app’s official website. It exists to describe the app and our services, publish our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, explain our text-messaging program, and host the plan pages our app sends to dinner guests. Everything described on this site — forming a group, getting a restaurant plan, RSVPing, and booking the table — happens inside the STANDING app, after you sign in with your phone number.

Our services

STANDING provides a complete recurring-dinner planning service through our iOS app, currently in private beta:

Recurring dinner planning

Create a standing group (“a Standing”) with your table of up to 8 people and a regular cadence — weekly, every two weeks, or monthly.

Restaurant proposals

For each dinner, STANDING proposes a restaurant, date, and time matched to your group’s cuisine preferences, budget, dietary needs, neighborhood, and past ratings.

RSVP coordination

Every guest gets the plan and confirms in a tap. STANDING sends reminders and nudges so no one has to chase replies in a group text.

Table booking

Once your group confirms, we book the table for you and send everyone the confirmed details.

Post-dinner ratings

After each dinner, your group rates the restaurant. STANDING learns what your table likes, so every next pick gets better.

Account verification by text

Accounts are verified with a one-time code sent by SMS to your phone number, and plan updates are delivered by text and push notification.

Frequently asked questions

What is STANDING?

A mobile app that plans recurring group dinners. You form a group in the app, set how often you want to meet and what you like, and the app proposes each dinner — restaurant, date, and time — coordinates everyone's RSVP, and books the table.

How do I get the app?

STANDING is an iPhone app, currently in private beta. Beta testers receive an invitation to install it through Apple's TestFlight app. A public App Store release will follow the beta.

What does STANDING cost?

The app is free to use during the private beta.

Why does STANDING send text messages?

Your phone number is your STANDING account. We text you a one-time code to verify it's you when you sign in, and we send dinner-plan updates (invitations, RSVPs, reminders, and changes) for plans you're part of. That's all — no marketing texts.

Who is behind STANDING?

STANDING is an independent software product based in Cambridge, Massachusetts, United States. See the company information in About STANDING above.

Our text-messaging program
