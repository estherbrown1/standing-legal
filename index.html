STANDING

Plans on repeat. Dinners with your people, every time.

STANDING is the app for recurring group dinners. You pick your people and your rhythm — every two weeks, every month, whatever works — and STANDING handles the rest: finding a restaurant everyone will love, locking in the plan, and booking the table for you.

How it works

  1. 1
    Form your Standing.
    Pick your table — the friends you want to see on repeat — and set your cadence and preferences.
  2. 2
    Get your plan.
    STANDING proposes a restaurant, date, and time that fits everyone's tastes and schedules.
  3. 3
    RSVP and show up.
    Everyone confirms in a tap. We book the table for you. After dinner, rate it — your next pick gets better.

Your table, your rules

Every Standing has its own preferences — cuisines you love, places to skip, the spot you always come back to. STANDING learns what your table likes and picks accordingly.

No more group-text chaos

No more 47-message threads to pick a night. STANDING proposes, everyone RSVPs, and the plan is set.
